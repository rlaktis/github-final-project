# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple command-line script to calculate simple interest based on the principal amount, annual rate of interest, and time period in years.

## Formula
Simple Interest = (P × T × R) / 100

Where:
- **P** = Principal amount
- **T** = Time period in years
- **R** = Annual interest rate

## Input
- Principal amount
- Rate of interest per year
- Time period in years

## Output
- Simple Interest
- Total amount
